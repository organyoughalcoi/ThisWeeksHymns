Hymns for Sunday 29 November 2026

The First Sunday of Advent

First Hymn

Hymn 132, Lo! he comes with clouds descending

Lo! he comes with clouds descending,
Once for favoured sinners slain.

Lo! he comes with clouds descending,
Once for favoured sinners slain;
Thousand thousand saints attending
Swell the triumph of his train:
Alleluia!
God appears, on earth to reign.

Second Hymn

Hymn 135: O come, O come Emmanuel

O come, O come, Emmanuel,
And ransom captive Israel.

O come, O come, Emmanuel,
And ransom captive Israel,
That mourns in lonely exile here,
Until the Son of God appear.

Historical Note: This hymn is based on the ancient “O Antiphons” of Advent.

Third Hymn

Hymn 136, On Jordan’s bank the Baptist’s cry

On Jordan’s bank the Baptist’s cry
Announces that the Lord is nigh.

On Jordan’s bank the Baptist’s cry
Announces that the Lord is nigh;
Awake and hearken, for he brings
Glad tidings of the King of kings.

Fourth Hymn

Hymn 126, Hark! a thrilling voice is sounding

Hark! a thrilling voice is sounding:
“Christ is nigh,” it seems to say.

Hark! a thrilling voice is sounding:
“Christ is nigh,” it seems to say;
“Cast away the dreams of darkness,
O ye children of the day!”
